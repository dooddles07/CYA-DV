# CYA Daily Verse — System Flow

**Kay Kristo Buong Buhay, Habambuhay!**

A guide to how the CYA Daily Verse platform works, from a person's first visit to becoming an
active part of the community. This document describes the **logical flow** of the product in plain
language. It is written for clients, leaders, and anyone being introduced to the platform. It does
not cover technical or engineering detail — that is kept in a separate document.

---

## 1. Overview

CYA Daily Verse is a daily-devotional platform for Christ's Youth in Action. It gives young people
a single place to receive a verse each day, reflect on it, search Scripture, follow reading plans,
pray together, and stay connected through community events.

It works like an app on any phone or computer, can be installed to the home screen, and even works
with a weak or missing connection. No download from an app store is required.

---

## 2. The Goal

The platform exists to help young people **build and keep a daily habit with God's Word** and to
**grow together as a community**. Everything in the product serves three simple aims:

- **Show up daily** — a fresh verse and a reason to come back every day.
- **Grow in faith** — tools to reflect, search, follow a plan, and go deeper.
- **Belong** — a shared prayer wall and events that turn individual reading into a community.

Success looks like a member who opens the platform each day, keeps a growing streak, saves the
verses that speak to them, prays for others, and shows up to events.

---

## 3. Who Uses It

The platform serves three kinds of people:

| Role | What they can do |
|------|------------------|
| **Visitor** | Anyone, not signed in. Can read the daily verse, search Scripture, browse devotionals, view the prayer wall and events. Free to explore before joining. |
| **Member** | A visitor who created an account. Everything a visitor can do, plus save verses, track streaks and progress, follow reading plans, post prayers, pray for others, RSVP to events, and receive daily reminders. |
| **Moderator / Admin** | Trusted CYA leaders. They keep the space safe and run the content — reviewing prayers, managing events and devotionals, and looking after member roles. |

---

## 4. The Big Picture

At its heart the platform follows one simple loop:

**Discover → Engage daily → Grow → Belong**

1. **Discover** — a person arrives, reads today's verse, and explores freely.
2. **Engage daily** — they come back each day, mark the verse as read, and build a streak.
3. **Grow** — they save verses, follow a reading plan, and reflect through devotionals.
4. **Belong** — they share and answer prayers, and join community events.

Members move deeper into this loop over time, while moderators quietly keep the whole space
healthy and safe in the background.

## 5. End-to-End Journeys

Each journey below is a simple story of what a person does and what happens in response.

### 5.1 First visit → becoming a member

1. A person arrives and immediately sees today's verse — no sign-up wall.
2. They explore the daily verse, search, devotionals, prayer wall, and events freely.
3. When they want to save progress or take part, they create an account with their name and email.
4. They confirm their email to unlock full participation (such as posting prayers).
5. They are now a member and can begin building their daily habit.

### 5.2 The daily devotional habit

1. Each day a new verse of the day is featured.
2. The member reads it, and can **listen** to it read aloud, **save** it, **copy**, or **share** it.
3. They tap **"I read today's verse"** to mark the day complete.
4. This grows their **streak** (days in a row) and earns points toward their level.
5. Optional daily challenges give extra ways to earn points and stay engaged.
6. Coming back the next day continues the streak; missing a day resets it — a gentle nudge to
   return.

### 5.3 Finding the right verse

Members and visitors can reach Scripture in several ways:

- **Search** — type a word, topic, or reference to find matching verses.
- **By mood** — choose how they feel (for example, "anxious" or "grateful") and receive a fitting
  verse.
- **By topic** — browse verses grouped into themes.
- **Surprise me** — get a random verse to discover something new.
- **Past days** — revisit the daily verses from recent weeks in the archive.

Recent searches and recently viewed verses are remembered on the person's own device for quick
return.

### 5.4 Reading plans

1. A member enrolls in a reading plan (for example, a chapter a day).
2. Each day they mark the day complete.
3. A simple week view shows their recent days, and an overall bar shows total progress.
4. They can switch plans, take a break, or start over at any time.

### 5.5 Devotionals

Short reflection articles pair a verse with a story and a practical "try this" step. Members can
read a featured devotional or browse the archive to go deeper than a single verse.

### 5.6 The prayer wall

1. A signed-in, confirmed member writes a prayer request.
2. They may post it under their name or **anonymously**.
3. The request appears on the shared prayer wall for the community to see.
4. Others tap **"I prayed"** to show support; a count reflects how many have prayed.
5. The space is watched over by moderators (see 5.10) so it stays safe and encouraging.

### 5.7 Events

1. Upcoming CYA events are shown with their artwork, date, time, and place.
2. A countdown shows how soon each event is.
3. A member taps **"I'm coming"** to RSVP, and can see how many others are attending.

### 5.8 Staying engaged

- **Daily reminders** — members can turn on a gentle daily notification so today's verse comes to
  them.
- **Install to phone** — the platform can be added to a phone's home screen and opened like any
  app, working even offline.

### 5.9 The personal dashboard

Each member has a personal space that brings their journey together:

- Their current **streak**, **level**, and **points**.
- **Saved verses** they can revisit anytime.
- **Achievements** earned along the way.
- **Reading-plan progress**.
- **Privacy and account controls** — including downloading a copy of their data or deleting their
  account.

### 5.10 The moderator / admin journey

Behind the scenes, trusted CYA leaders keep the platform healthy:

- **Prayer moderation** — review the prayer wall and hide anything inappropriate. Posts are only
  hidden, never permanently erased, and a badge highlights new posts from the last 24 hours.
- **Events** — create, update, publish, and remove events, and upload their artwork.
- **Devotionals** — write and manage the reflection articles.
- **Member roles** — grant or remove moderator access for other leaders.

This work is done in a separate, private area of the platform and is invisible to regular members.

---

## 6. Guiding Principles

The whole flow is built on a few commitments:

- **A daily habit first** — the product is designed around coming back every day.
- **Community, not just content** — prayers and events turn reading into belonging.
- **A safe space** — the prayer wall is moderated, and posting is limited to confirmed members.
- **Works for everyone** — usable on any phone, installable, and functional even offline.
- **Respects privacy** — members can export or delete their own data at any time.
