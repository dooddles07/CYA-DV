# Code of Conduct

## Our pledge

CYA Daily Verse is built by and for *Christ's Youth in Action*. We pledge to make participation in
this project a welcoming, safe, and harassment-free experience for everyone, regardless of age,
background, ability, or experience level.

## Our standards

Examples of behavior that contributes to a positive environment:

- Showing kindness, patience, and respect toward others.
- Giving and gracefully accepting constructive feedback.
- Focusing on what is best for the community and its members.
- Serving one another in the spirit of the ministry.

Unacceptable behavior includes:

- Harassment, insults, or derogatory comments.
- Publishing others' private information without permission.
- Sexualized language or imagery, or unwelcome advances.
- Any conduct that would reasonably be considered inappropriate in a ministry setting.

## Scope

This Code of Conduct applies within all project spaces — the repository, issues, pull requests,
reviews — and in any public space when an individual is representing the project or community.

## Enforcement

Instances of abusive or unacceptable behavior may be reported to the project maintainers (CYA
leadership). All complaints will be reviewed and investigated promptly and fairly. Maintainers are
obligated to respect the privacy and security of the reporter.

Maintainers who do not follow or enforce this Code of Conduct in good faith may face temporary or
permanent repercussions as determined by CYA leadership.
